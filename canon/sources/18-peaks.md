One thing I'd flag: Episode 24 currently dominates all three categories. That's a powerful finale, but the season needs at least one earlier scene that genuinely rivals it, rather than saving every extraordinary moment for the end.
I'd target Episode 12 for the greatest pre-finale character peak and Episode 18 for the greatest strategic revelation. That gives the season three distinct high points instead of one enormous ending.
