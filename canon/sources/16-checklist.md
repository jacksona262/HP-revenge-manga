The six things I'd prioritize




1. Character design bible
We need consistent hairstyles, facial features, uniforms, heights, expressions, scars, and silhouettes. Every major character should be recognizable even without dialogue or color.
The existing gang posters are our starting references, but they're not yet complete character sheets.





2. World map and gang territories
Where is HP headquarters? Where do Branch's 389 members operate? How far apart are the six gangs?
We need school locations, hangouts, meeting spots, neutral areas, and travel distances so characters don't magically appear everywhere.





3. Chapter and panel structure
Our 24 episodes are currently story units, not actual manga chapters.
I'd target approximately 35–45 chapters for Season 1, with 18–24 pages per chapter. Major fights and emotional turning points get more pages.





4. Fight choreography rules
Jackson and Jack are nearly equal, but they need visually different fighting styles. The manga should communicate their intelligence and technique through positioning, timing, expressions, and movement rather than relying on narration.





5. Dialogue and emotional pacing
We need each character to sound different. Jack should not talk like Marcus. Isaac should not talk like Ren.
We also need comedy, ordinary school life, silence, and emotional buildup between major events.





6. Production pipeline
We need a repeatable workflow: script → page breakdown → panel thumbnails → consistent character artwork → lettering → continuity review → final pages.
Without this, we'll end up with beautiful individual images that don't read like a coherent manga.
One more issue: Our current canon needs a cleanup
We've made some decisions that don't completely agree with one another yet.
For example, Jack and Jason appear in both HP and Tsukabi, which we've explained through their eventual departure. But the precise dates and some character heights still need reconciling.
Likewise, Tsukabi begins with 124 members under Dev's network, so we need to establish exactly when that group becomes a formal gang.
And our 24-episode outline has a solid central conflict, but the story needs more personal motivations for secondary characters before it becomes a full script.
These aren't huge problems. They're exactly the kind of issues a canon bible should resolve before production.
What I'd do next
My order would be:
Finish all 60 character identities and personalities. 
Establish the physical world and gang territories. 
Lock the chronological timeline and relationship history. 
Convert the 24-episode outline into a chapter-by-chapter manga plan. 
Build character reference sheets and art rules. 
Produce one complete pilot chapter and evaluate whether the pages actually work. 
The single most important missing piece is character consistency. If Jackson looks different every chapter, or Isaac suddenly acts nothing like himself, the entire manga loses credibility.
And there's one creative decision I'd make early: black-and-white manga with selective color for covers and special illustrations, rather than attempting full-color pages for everything. It would give HP: Revenge a more cohesive identity and make a long series much more practical to produce.
We're building toward an actual readable manga, not just a collection of character posters.
