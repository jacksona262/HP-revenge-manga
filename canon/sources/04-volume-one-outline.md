Volume one outline 


HP: REVENGE
Episode 1 — The Promise
Complete manga storyboard script · 50 pages · Approximately 220 panels · Chapters 1–2
We're making Episode 1 a full-length manga opening, not a rushed introduction to six gangs. The reader should finish these 50 pages understanding why Jackson and Jack are so close, why HP exists, and why people would follow them.
Episode 1's job: Make the reader love the friendship before anything threatens it.
The opening should feel like an actual school day that gradually reveals a much larger world. No supernatural powers, no giant exposition dump, and no premature betrayal.

Manga production notes
This episode should be visually quieter than later installments. Jackson and Jack's friendship needs room to feel natural, including jokes, boredom, disagreements, and ordinary conversations.
The fight should be short relative to the full episode, emphasizing Jack's exceptional composure without turning him into a supernatural fighter. Jackson's intelligence should come through his observations and decisions rather than characters constantly announcing how smart he is.
One important distinction: This is a 50-page storyboard script, not yet 50 finished illustrated manga pages. Each numbered page has a panel plan and dialogue, ready for the next stage of visual production.
