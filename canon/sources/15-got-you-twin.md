2. Major fights — full outcomes and consequences
Fight 4 — Shiranui vs. Kurogetsu
Episode 4 · Chapters 7–8
Cause: Escalating territorial pressure and conflicting accounts of an earlier incident.  
Key figures: Kai, Ren, London Mincey, Kade Mercer.  
Outcome: Neither gang establishes a decisive victory.  
Consequence: Both leaders recognize that someone benefits from their rivalry. Marcus Vale offers a council as the supposed solution.  
Narrative purpose: Establish that even two powerful gangs cannot control every conflict through force.  
Fight 6 — HP vs. Kurogetsu
Episode 8 · Chapters 15–16
Cause: Jack acts without Jackson's authorization after an escalating dispute involving Kade.  
Key figures: Jack, Jackson, Kade, Luke.  
Outcome: HP achieves a local tactical victory.  
Consequence: Jackson removes Jack's operational authority. HP membership drops from 106 to 94.  
Narrative purpose: The first irreversible fracture in Jackson and Jack's leadership partnership.  
Fight 7 — Branch vs. Kurogetsu
Episode 10 · Chapters 19–20
Cause: Manipulated information creates a confrontation between two otherwise disciplined organizations.  
Key figures: Bryson, Isaac, Ren, Kade.  
Featured matchup: Isaac vs. Kade.  
Outcome: Bryson and Ren stop the conflict before either side secures a decisive victory.  
Consequence: Branch falls from 354 to 341 members; Kurogetsu falls from 478 to 462.  
Narrative purpose: Demonstrate Bryson's authority, Isaac's unpredictability, and the cost of false information.  
Fight 8 — Tsukabi's first victory
Episode 12 · Chapters 23–24
Cause: Jack takes command of Dev's existing network after leaving HP and faces an immediate challenge to its legitimacy.  
Key figures: Jack, Dev, Jason Schmidt, Max Keller.  
Outcome: Tsukabi wins.  
Consequence: Tsukabi grows from 161 to 210 members. HP falls from 86 to 59 as Jack's departure destabilizes the organization.  
Narrative purpose: Establish Tsukabi as a genuine sixth power rather than merely a splinter faction.  
Fight 11 — Kurogetsu vs. Tsukabi
Episode 16 · Chapters 31–32
Cause: Competition for influence and rising tension between two intimidating leaders.  
Featured matchup: Ren vs. Jack.  
Outcome: Jack and Ren remain competitive without a conclusive individual result. Bryson and Kai intervene to stop further escalation.  
Consequence: Kurogetsu declines from 483 to 470 members; Tsukabi declines from 257 to 246.  
Narrative purpose: Prove Jack can contend with the strongest rival leaders while showing that his new gang is still vulnerable.  
Fight 13 — Kaien vs. Branch
Episode 19 · Chapters 37–38
Cause: Kaien's manipulation is exposed, and tensions between its remaining loyalists and Branch escalate.  
Featured matchup: Isaac Diaz vs. Nolan Pierce.  
Outcome: Branch wins the larger confrontation.  
Consequence: Branch drops from 371 to 358 members; Kaien drops from 235 to 207.  
Narrative purpose: The beginning of Kaien's collapse and a major demonstration of Bryson and Isaac's partnership.  
Fight 14 — Kurogetsu vs. Shiranui rematch
Episode 20 · Chapters 39–40
Cause: Unresolved grievances from their earlier confrontation.  
Featured matchup: Ren vs. Kai.  
Outcome: Both leaders voluntarily withdraw rather than continue escalating.  
Consequence: Kurogetsu falls from 466 to 445 members; Shiranui falls from 295 to 283.  
Narrative purpose: Ren and Kai demonstrate that leadership requires knowing when to stop.  
Fight 15 — HP vs. Tsukabi
Episode 21 · Chapters 41–42
Cause: Jack refuses Jackson's proposed peace agreement.  
Key figures: Jackson, Jack, Luke, Dev.  
Outcome: Tsukabi gains a limited territorial advantage; Jackson and Jack do not have their definitive duel.  
Consequence: HP falls from 83 to 75 members; Tsukabi falls from 267 to 256 despite its local advantage.  
Narrative purpose: Show that winning territory does not necessarily mean winning strategically.  
Fight 17 — HP vs. Tsukabi rematch
Episode 23 · Chapters 45–46
Cause: The discovery and circulation of stolen HP contingency plans destroys the remaining trust between the gangs.  
Key figures: Jackson, Jack, Luke, Dev, Jason.  
Outcome: HP successfully holds its position, but neither organization secures a decisive overall victory.  
Consequence: HP falls from 79 to 70 members; Tsukabi falls from 262 to 247.  
Narrative purpose: Bring the conflict to the point where the two leaders finally agree to settle their personal dispute.  
Fight 18 — Six-gang confrontation
Episode 24 · Chapters 47–48
Cause: Marcus's remaining network attempts to exploit the final breakdown of cooperation.  
Key figures: Jackson, Jack, Bryson, Isaac, Ren, Kai, Adrian.  
Outcome: The six organizations prevent Marcus's remaining loyalists from restoring his influence.  
Consequence: The gangs begin negotiating a decentralized peace arrangement.  
Narrative purpose: Resolve the season's larger political conflict without pretending that every personal grievance has disappeared.

Episode 24 · Final confrontation
Winner: Jackson Anderson, by an exceptionally narrow margin.
Thematic structure:
Opening: Both fighters understand each other's habits because they spent years together.  
Middle: Their familiarity forces each to adapt, making the duel as much psychological as physical.  
Turning point: Jackson anticipates a decision Jack would once have made differently, revealing how much both have changed.  
Resolution: Jackson narrowly wins without humiliating Jack.  
Aftermath: Jackson offers his hand. Jack acknowledges him but remains the leader of Tsukabi.  
Canon restriction: Jack never becomes stronger than Jackson. His 99.5% strength means their confrontation is exceptionally close, not predetermined or effortless.
Production note: The previously proposed 45–55-page duel cannot fit entirely inside Chapters 47–48 if both chapters are standard length. During the final chapter allocation, the confrontation must begin earlier or the finale must receive extended chapters. The story outcome remains unchanged.
