CHAPTER 1 — THE BOYS UNDER THE TREE
Pages 1–25 · Approximately 110 panels
Page 1 — The tree
Layout: One full-page illustration.
A sprawling oak tree stands behind an older school building. Late afternoon sunlight filters through its leaves. Two younger boys sit beneath it, their schoolbags beside them.
The taller boy is Jackson. Beside him sits Jack, whose shoes are muddy from running around.
Neither wears a gang uniform.
Caption: Five years earlier.
"Do you think we'll still be friends when we're older?" - said Jack
No response yet. The entire page holds on the question.
